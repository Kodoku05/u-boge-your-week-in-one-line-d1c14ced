## Pairwise selection

I compared every survivor using the published order: week summary first, joke second, distinctive weekly detail third, compression fourth, freshness fifth. Lines 3 and 6 have strong details but their turns depend on explaining the mismatch between a paper race or worthless token and an ordinary financial metaphor. Lines 2, 5 and 7 resolve weakly: stopping, career comparison and wallet connection are familiar punchlines. Line 1's zoom-in is unclear, and line 8's "Again?" is disconnected from the boss's real question. Lines 4 and 9 have vivid scenes, but adult supervision and the mouse-as-portfolio gag are more familiar; line 9 also invents a request. Line 10 wins the close pairings: it combines the true 30 entries and one-cent result with the boss's actual question about getting rich, then turns it into a deadpan decimal edit. Its specific accounting joke and compact wording beat the alternatives on the laugh, detail and freshness without using a stock saying.
